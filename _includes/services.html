How I Help

Medication

Medication planning, reconciliation & reviews with adherence support through education, check-ins, and reminders.

Delivery & Transportation

Pickup and delivery of prescription refills & assist with transportation to doctor visits.

Costing

Insurance-based cost savings review and enrollment in Manufacturer Assistance Programs.

Doctor & Family Liaison

Act as a health proxy with your medical team and liaison with family members on current status of health and medications.

Independence

In addition to medication reviews, we offer practical help with organizing your home, setting up electronic bill pay, and preparing healthy meals — giving you or your loved one the tools to live independently

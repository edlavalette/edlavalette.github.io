Real Stories, Real Support

A client reached out when he wanted to travel to meet his new grandchild but was the primary support for his sister at home. During his time away, I stepped in to provide weekly pill box setup, regular check-ins, and coordinated additional support with the local Council on Aging, so he could travel with peace of mind, knowing she was safe and cared for.

Real-Life Support, When It Matters Most

I was asked to visit a homebound senior managing a large and complex medication regimen. During my visit, I reviewed her medications, asked detailed questions about her health and routines, and reconciled her list with her pharmacy. Afterward, I created a personalized medication assessment for her care team, identifying duplicate therapies, potential interactions, and opportunities to simplify her regimen. Her team welcomed the insights, helping improve coordination, safety, and overall confidence in her care.

Bringing Clarity to Complex Medication Regimens

After a recent hospitalization and new diagnosis, a patient needed help understanding her medications and how they supported her health. She was feeling overwhelmed and unsure what to continue taking. During her home visit, I discovered she was still taking medications that had been discontinued, along with expired supplements. I coordinated with her provider to reconcile her medication list, clarified her regimen, and explained each condition and medication in simple terms. I also organized her medications and set up ongoing refill support. She felt confident, organized, and better equipped to stay on track and avoid another hospitalization.

FREE One-on-One Medication Review

Bring in all of your medications for a free 30-minute private consultation with SoCo Rx Consultants. Pre-registration with each Council on Aging is required. Please visit or call these two sites to reserve your appointment.

Dartmouth Council On Aging

2nd Thursday of the Month


Mattapoisett Council On Aging

3rd Monday of the Month


Please bring:

  • Prescription medications
  • Over-the-counter medicines
  • Vitamins and supplements
  • Creams and ointments
  • Inhalers
  • Eye drops or other medical products you use regularly

Bringing the actual medication bottles and products is much more helpful than bringing only a medication list. This allows for a more complete and accurate review.

During your visit, we will:

  • Review your medications for safety and possible drug interactions
  • Check for proper dosages
  • Help you understand what each medication is for
  • Discuss the best time to take medications (morning, evening, with food, etc.)
  • Answer your medication and wellness questions in easy-to-understand language
  • Provide tips to help simplify your medication routine and improve confidence with your care
